Karam ES 61 Welding Face Shield

Brand: KARAM

Product Overview

The Karam ES 61 is a robust welding face shield engineered for comprehensive protection during intensive welding operations such as ARC, TIG, and MIG. Featuring a heat-resistant polypropylene co-polymer shell and a convenient flip-up window, it offers seamless transitions between welding and inspection.

Key Features

  • Flip-Up Design: Innovative flip-up front window allows users to inspect work without removing the entire shield, enhancing productivity.
  • Customizable Fit: Equipped with a ratchet-type adjustment harness and a 4-position headband to easily control the stop angle.
  • Thermal Protection: Durable polypropylene shell protects against IR radiation, sparks, and extreme temperatures (-50°C to +80°C).
  • Interchangeable Lens: Compatible with various IR lenses (IR 5, IR 11) to adapt to different welding intensities.

Technical Specifications

Certification / Standard EN 175:1997
Shell Material Polypropylene Co-polymer
Lens Material High-Impact Polycarbonate
Window Size 100mm x 75mm
Harness Type Ratchet Adjustment with Anti-Sweat Pad

Ideal for Use

ARC Welding, TIG Welding, MIG Welding, Heavy Fabrication, and Steel Processing Plants.
