Frontier FT801(UL)(GY)(14) Gloves

Brand: KARAM (Frontier)

Product Overview

The Frontier FT801(UL)(GY)(14) are robust unlined grey split leather gloves engineered for superior hand protection and durability in high-temperature tasks. Featuring an ergonomic wing thumb design and a secure 14-inch gauntlet cuff, these gloves are a trusted choice for demanding industrial applications.

Key Features

  • Premium Leather: Crafted from good quality split leather for excellent puncture and abrasion resistance.
  • Heat Resistance: Certified to provide heat resistance up to 100°C for up to 15 seconds.
  • Ergonomic Cut: Features a wing thumb and gunn cut design, enhancing dexterity and reducing seam exposure in the palm area.
  • Extended Protection: Equipped with a 14-inch gauntlet cuff to protect the wrist and lower forearm from sparks and debris.

Certification / Standard EN 388:2016, EN 407:2020
Material Construction High-Quality Split Leather
Pattern / Style Gunn Cut, Wing Thumb
Cuff Style 14-Inch Gauntlet Cuff
Color / Lining Grey (GY), Unlined (UL)
