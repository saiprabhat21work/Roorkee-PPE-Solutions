Karam FS05 Workman Safety Shoes

Brand: KARAM EN ISO 20345:2022 · S1 · IS 15298 (Part-02)

Product Overview

The Karam FS05 is a robust, entry-level safety shoe designed for the demanding workman. Built with a full grain leather upper and a single-density injection-moulded PU outsole, it delivers reliable foot protection with all-day comfort at an excellent price point.

Key Features

  • Carbon Steel Toe Cap: Protects against falling objects up to 200 Joules of impact energy.
  • Full Grain Leather Upper: Offers superior durability, abrasion resistance, and a professional appearance.
  • Single Density PU Sole: Injection-moulded for high energy absorption and long-lasting wear resistance.
  • Slip-Resistant Outsole: Cleated tread pattern provides excellent grip on wet and uneven surfaces.
  • Antistatic: Dissipates static electricity build-up to reduce the risk of electrostatic discharge.
