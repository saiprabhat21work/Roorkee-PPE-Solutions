Karam ES 71 Helmet-Mounted Welding Shield

Brand: KARAM

Product Overview

The Karam ES 71 is a high-performance, helmet-mountable welding face shield. Designed for heavy industrial applications like ARC, TIG, and MIG welding, this shield seamlessly integrates with standard safety helmets to provide simultaneous head, eye, and face protection against impacts and IR radiation.

Key Features

  • Helmet Integration: Easily attaches to standard safety helmets (Shelmet, Sheltek, Shelblast) via adapter clamps.
  • Flip-Up Window: Allows the user to lift the welding lens to perform non-welding tasks without removing the entire shield or helmet.
  • Impact Protection: The wrap-around design protects the face from high-speed particle impacts (tested up to 45 m/s).
  • Electrical Insulation: Rigorously tested for electrical insulation properties to ensure complete worker safety in complex environments.

Technical Specifications

Certification / Standard EN 175:1997
Shell Material High-Impact Polypropylene Co-Polymer
Lens Material High-Impact Polycarbonate (Supports IR 05/IR 11)
Viewing Window Size 100 mm x 75 mm
Mounting Helmet Mounted (30.0 mm accessory slot)

Ideal for Use

ARC Welding, TIG Welding, MIG Welding, Construction Sites, Heavy Fabrication, and Integrated Safety Environments.
