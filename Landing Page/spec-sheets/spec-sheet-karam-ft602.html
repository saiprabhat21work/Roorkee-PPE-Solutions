Frontier FT602(70) Knitted Gloves

Brand: KARAM (Frontier)

Product Overview

The Frontier FT602(70) are lightweight, 7-gauge cotton knitted gloves designed for general-purpose handling. Made from natural cotton fibers, these uncoated gloves deliver exceptional breathability, comfort, and a seamless liner that prevents skin irritation during long shifts.

Key Features

  • Natural Cotton Fibers: Offers great comfort, sweat absorption, and breathability.
  • Seamless Construction: Designed without irritating seams to maximize wearer comfort over extended periods.
  • Ambidextrous Design: Symmetrical pattern allows the gloves to be worn on either the left or right hand seamlessly.
  • Snug Fit: Features a knit wrist with a finished hem to securely lock the glove in place.
