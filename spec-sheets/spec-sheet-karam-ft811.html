Frontier FT811(P)(GY)(14) Gloves

Brand: KARAM (Frontier)

Product Overview

The Frontier FT811(P)(GY)(14) are premium heavy-duty split leather welding gloves. What sets these gloves apart is their specialized welted joints, designed to provide exceptional structural strength, drastically reduce seam tearing, and offer enhanced overall protection during the most demanding tasks.

Key Features

  • Welted Joints: Built with reinforced welted seams to enhance durability and prevent seam burnout or tearing.
  • Premium Leather: Crafted from high-quality split leather for superior puncture and abrasion resistance.
  • Heat Resistance: Certified to provide heat resistance up to 100°C for up to 15 seconds.
  • Ergonomic Cut: Features a wing thumb and gunn cut design for excellent dexterity, along with a secure 14-inch gauntlet cuff.

Technical Specifications

Certification / Standard EN 388:2016, EN 407:2020
Material Construction High-Quality Split Leather
Seam Design Reinforced Welted Joints (Premium Version)
Pattern / Style Gunn Cut, Wing Thumb
Cuff Style 14-Inch Gauntlet Cuff

Ideal for Use

Welding, Heavy Metal Fabrication, Smelting, Material Handling, Maintenance Operations, and Construction Applications.
