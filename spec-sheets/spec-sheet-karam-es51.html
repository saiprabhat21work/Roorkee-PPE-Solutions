Karam ES 51 Face Shield

Brand: KARAM

Product Overview

The Karam ES 51 is a premium helmet-mounted face shield designed to provide complete frontal and lateral facial protection. It features a high-impact polycarbonate visor that easily mounts onto standard KARAM safety helmets (Sheltek, Shelblast, etc.) to safeguard against flying debris and splashes.

Key Features

  • Helmet Integration: Designed specifically for seamless mounting onto industrial safety helmets with easy flip-up functionality.
  • Optical Clarity: Class 1 optical grade polycarbonate visor ensures distortion-free, clear vision.
  • Maximum Protection: Offers high resistance against heavy impact from high-speed particles.
  • Removable Visor: The shield can be easily removed and replaced when damaged without needing to replace the entire bracket assembly.

Technical Specifications

Certification / Standard EN 166:2001, ANSI Z87.1-2020, IS 8521-1:2022
Visor Material High-Impact Polycarbonate
Optical Class Class 1
Visor Dimensions 203.2 mm x 393.7 mm (8" x 15.5")
Mounting Helmet Mounted (KARAM series compatible)

Ideal for Use

Heavy Engineering, Construction Sites, Grinding, Welding, Metal Processing, and Petrochemical Plants.
