Product Overview

The Karam ES 001 is a premium ultra-lightweight frameless safety spectacle designed for industrial environments. Its wrap-around design and flexible temples ensure a secure and comfortable fit, offering outstanding protection against high-speed flying particles.

Key Features

  • Two distinct variants available: Clear lens for standard indoor lighting, and Smoke lens for outdoor glare reduction.
  • Lightweight (approx. 26 grams), frameless, and wrap-around design.
  • High-impact-resistant, optical class 1 polycarbonate lens.
  • Hard-coated lens for superior scratch resistance and 99.9% UV protection.
  • Tested for robustness, disinfection, and resistance to corrosion & ignition.
