Frontier FT401 Safety Gloves

Brand: KARAM (Frontier)

Product Overview

The Frontier FT401 are high-performance green safety gloves featuring a 10-gauge cotton composite shell coated with a latex crinkle finish on the palm. These gloves are designed to provide excellent grip in both dry and wet conditions while maximizing wearer comfort.

Key Features

  • Crinkle Latex Coating: The textured latex palm coating ensures exceptional grip and strong mechanical performance in slippery or wet environments.
  • Breathable Open Back: An uncoated back design drastically reduces perspiration build-up and keeps hands cool.
  • 10-Gauge Cotton Liner: The cotton composite liner delivers excellent flexibility and ergonomic comfort.
  • Secure Fit: Features an elasticated knit wrist to prevent dirt and debris from entering the glove.
