Karam RF01 FFP1 Respirator

Brand: KARAM

Product Overview

The Karam RF01 is an FFP1-rated disposable face respirator mask engineered for high-efficiency particulate filtration. It features a multi-layer design with electrostatically charged filters to provide up to 12 hours of respiratory protection in dust-prone environments.

Key Features

  • Soft, adjustable ear loops for a comfortable fit.
  • An aluminium nose clip in this dust safety mask enhances sealing.
  • FFP1 S mask has an electrostatically charged filter for improved filtration.
  • Water-repellent outer layer.
  • Maintains shape even with prolonged use.
