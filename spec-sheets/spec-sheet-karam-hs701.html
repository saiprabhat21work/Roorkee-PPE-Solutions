Karam HS 701 (22) TH Kevlar Sleeves

Brand: KARAM

Product Overview

The Karam HS 701 (22) TH are highly protective arm sleeves designed for hazardous environments. Crafted from 100% genuine Kevlar® fiber, these two-ply knitted sleeves offer excellent resistance against cuts and contact heat while remaining lightweight and non-irritating.

Key Features

  • Premium Material: Made entirely from 100% genuine Kevlar® fiber for maximum durability and protection.
  • Thumb Hole Design (TH): Features a convenient thumb hole ensuring a snug fit, preventing the sleeve from rolling up and improving wrist protection.
  • Thermal Resistance: Provides certified protection against thermal risks, passing the EN 407:2020 contact heat test.
  • 22-Inch Length: Extended length ensures full forearm and bicep coverage.
