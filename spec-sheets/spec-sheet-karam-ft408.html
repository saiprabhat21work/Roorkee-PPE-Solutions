Frontier FT408 Safety Gloves

Brand: KARAM (Frontier)

Product Overview

The Frontier FT408 are durable and versatile safety gloves featuring a 13-gauge polyester shell with a grey nitrile palm coating. Engineered for versatility, they deliver exceptional comfort, dexterity, and excellent grip in both dry and slightly oily environments.

Key Features

  • Nitrile Palm Coating: Provides good resistance to abrasion and maintains excellent grip even when handling slightly oily or greasy parts.
  • Lightweight Dexterity: The 13-gauge seamless polyester shell ensures a lightweight feel, allowing for precision handling and maximum dexterity.
  • Breathable Comfort: Uncoated back allows heat to escape, keeping hands comfortable during extended wear.
  • Secure Fit: Features an elasticated knit wrist to securely lock the glove in place and prevent debris ingress.
